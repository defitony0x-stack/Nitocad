Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 15, 2026

This document is a plain-language draft and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

1. Who we are

NitoCAD is operated by Anthony Amos ("we"). This policy explains what personal information we handle when you use the Service at de-nitocad-updated.vercel.app. Questions and requests: Nitocadai@gmail.com or elijahanthony005@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email and name if you sign in with Google, and your public wallet address if you connect a wallet.
  • Your content: prompts, uploaded files, generated geometry, parameters, exports, projects and feedback.
  • Payment records: credit balances, purchase amounts, transaction hashes and wallet addresses. We do not handle your wallet keys.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, timestamps, error logs and security events.

Please keep personal information and confidential material out of prompts and files unless you need it there.

3. How we use it

  • To run the Service: generate parts, store projects and exports, and manage credits.
  • To keep it secure and stop abuse.
  • To support you, answer requests and handle refunds.
  • To fix problems and improve reliability.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Who else handles your data

To provide the Service we use outside providers, including an AI language-model provider (currently DeepSeek) that receives your prompt text to extract design parameters, Google for sign-in, and cloud hosting for the website and the backend (currently Vercel and Railway). These providers handle data under their own terms and retention settings, which we do not control, so do not submit confidential designs unless you are comfortable with that. If we add a provider that changes how your data is handled, we will update this page.

5. Blockchain records

Payments and some records use a public blockchain. Wallet addresses, transaction hashes and timestamps may be visible to anyone and cannot be deleted by us, even if you delete your account.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser storage for sign-in sessions and preferences, and to protect the Service. You can clear them in your browser, but some features may stop working.

7. How long we keep it

We keep information while your account is active and for as long as needed for security, accounting, legal and dispute purposes. Deleted data can remain in backups for a limited time. Ask for deletion at the email above.

8. Security and transfers

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, such as access controls and encrypted connections. No online service is completely secure. Our providers may process data in other countries, and we rely on the safeguards the law requires for such transfers.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict some uses, withdraw consent, or receive a copy of your data. Where the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 applies, you can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Email us to make a request; we may need to confirm who you are first.

10. Children

The Service is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will act on it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date below when we do.
