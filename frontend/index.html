How it works

No CAD or engineering experience needed.

1

Start with your idea

Describe it in everyday language, or upload a sketch, drawing or existing design.

2

Answer a few questions

NitoCAD asks for the details it needs, like dimensions, materials and how it should work. You decide the requirements.

3

Watch it take shape

Your description becomes a real 3D design, built in stages you can follow.

4

Make changes

Want it bigger, lighter or different? Change it without losing earlier versions.

5

Check the design

It is checked against your requirements, with clear PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN results.

6

Get your files

Export STEP, STL, IGES, DXF and PDF, ready for your next workflow.

Checked against your requirements

Every revision is checked, and a result is never rounded up.

Pass

The requirement is met and NitoCAD measured it.

Fail

The requirement is not met, so you know what to change.

Unknown

NitoCAD could not measure it, or an input is missing. It is never shown as a pass.

Requirements stay attached

They are re-checked on every version, so a change cannot quietly break one.

Revision history

Every change is a new revision. The evidence for a revision stays with that revision.

Manufacturing checks

Fits and tolerances, manufacturability, overlap and mass, using typical shop guidelines.

Your numbers stay yours

Values you type are kept as written. If NitoCAD sees a problem, it flags it and leaves the value alone.

Six engineering disciplines

Mechanical

Full checks

Machine parts, mounts, enclosures, shafts, gears and assemblies.

Checks use typical shop guidelines, not your shop’s limits.

Automotive

Components

Wheel hubs, brake rotors, control arms, steering knuckles, pistons and flywheels.

A recognised bolt pattern is not a fitment claim.

Aerospace

Review-ready only

Airfoils, stability, engine cycle, mass and CG, with a signed review trail.

Ends at “ready for engineering review”. Never certification.

Civil

Screening level

Survey surfaces, alignments, earthworks, drainage and retaining wall stability.

Screening level. The limits are yours to set.

Buildings

Conceptual rules

Layouts, structure, schedules, drawing sets and rule checks.

Conceptual rules only. Never reports a full code pass.

Circuits

Light rule check

Place and route a board, then export a KiCad project and Gerbers.

A light design-rule check. Run a full one before you order boards.

Questions

Will it tell me when it is not sure?
Yes. Every check answers PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN. UNKNOWN means NitoCAD could not measure it or an input was missing, and it is never shown as a pass.
Can it certify my design?
No. Aerospace results stop at “ready for engineering review”, civil checks are screening level and building rules are conceptual. A licensed engineer must verify anything that gets built.
Does it change the numbers I type?
No. Every value you type is kept exactly as written. If NitoCAD sees a problem with a dimension, it flags it and leaves the value alone.
Is the preview really free?
Yes. The preview is a free STL mesh and needs no account. The STEP, IGES, DXF and PDF files are generated in the Workspace and included in every plan.
What if a build fails?
You are not charged. A failed generation is refunded.
Can I start from my own file?
Yes. Type the part in plain English, or attach a STEP file, a DXF or a sketch.
Which formats can I export?
STEP, STL, IGES, DXF, PDF, GLB, 3MF and URDF.
How do I pay?
Pro and Max are billed monthly in US dollars by card. See full pricing.
